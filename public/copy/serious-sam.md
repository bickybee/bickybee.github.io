# The Project

During my time at Behaviour Interactive, I worked on the early development of the recently announced Serious Sam: Shatterverse! 

# My Contributions

I ramped up quickly on Unreal Engine 5, its built-in networking and replication systems, as well as the Gameplay Ability System (GAS), which was fairly new to the engine at the time.

In the early prototyping stages my tasks varied widely depending on prototyping needs. Later on I found my niche in enemy behaviours: I collaborated closely with technical designers on building out custom Behaviour Tree functionality.

I'll fill this page out more after this game is released(supposedly 2026)!
